About the Ratings

  • Polls rated: final-matchup polls fielded in the last 45 days before mayoral elections and the last 50 days before presidential elections, as listed on Chinese Wikipedia's election pages.
  • Avg. miss: in mayoral races, the average gap in points between the poll and the result on the DPP's share of the two main candidates' vote, with undecided respondents allocated by the county's partisan lean. In presidential races, the gap on the DPP–KMT margin, with undecided respondents split evenly between the two main candidates.
  • vs. rivals: how much closer (negative) or farther (positive) a pollster was than the other polls of the same race. This separates a pollster's own accuracy from years when every pollster missed together, as in 2020.
  • Lean: a pollster's average tilt relative to other polls of the same mayoral race, after shrinking small samples toward zero.
  • Grade: A–F by combined "vs. rivals" score across both kinds of races, for pollsters with at least rated polls. Others show history without a grade.
  • Sponsors: polls commissioned by a party or campaign are labeled. Names are combined where the same operation publishes under several (for example ET東森 and ETtoday; 震撼世代傳媒 and 震傳媒; 鉅聞天下 and 皮爾森數據).
  • Limits: many pollsters have only a few rated polls, and methodology (landline vs. mobile, online panels) is not graded because it is rarely documented.
Ratings computed from Central Election Commission results and Wikipedia's poll listings.
