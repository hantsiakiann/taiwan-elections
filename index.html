Method

How the lean numbers are calculated

Two-camp lean. Every candidate is assigned to Pan-Green, Pan-Blue or Other. We take the Green share of the two-camp vote — Green ÷ (Green + Blue), ignoring Other — for the nation and for the area, and the lean is the area's two-camp Green margin minus the national one that year, in points. Measuring against the same election's national result cancels out how big the third-party vote was that year, so all eight elections are comparable even though the field changes (James Soong 2000/2012/2016/2020, Li Ao 2000, Lin Yang-kang 1996, Ko Wen-je 2024). Blue-aligned minor candidates fold into Pan-Blue; non-aligned ones (Ko 2024, Chen Li-an 1996, Hsu Hsin-liang 2000) drop out. The toggles above reclassify the ambiguous ones.

DPP / KMT / TPP lean. The same relative idea with three buckets: DPP camp, KMT camp (including the New Party and James Soong / PFP every time he ran) and TPP camp (Ko Wen-je, 2024 only). Non-aligned independents are excluded. For a place and year we take each bucket's share of the bucket total, do the same nationally, and report the differences (they sum to zero). Because the TPP fielded a presidential candidate only in 2024, this view is an ordinary DPP-KMT lean for 1996–2020 and three-way only in 2024. The two-camp toggles above do not affect this view.
